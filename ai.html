AI

I like to own my creative process, so I am not interested in leaving my work in the black box of generative tools. AI is advanced now, which means I can build it into my pipeline: it takes over repetitive tasks and speeds up the work.
When I am stuck on an idea, I use it for a quick brainstorm, for example trying ten different colour options in minutes. Then I go back to more manual work, because creative work stays creative work. I am curious about every new tool, and I like to open the box and understand how it works, which is why I explored ComfyUI.
